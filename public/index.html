The electric field in 3D

Field lines do not lie in a flat page: they fill the space around the charges. Place charges and charged objects on the table and look at their field from every side. Use the VR tab in a headset browser (for example Meta Quest), AR on a tablet or a headset with passthrough, or stay in the browser.

Explore the space

Try one charge on its own; a positive and a negative charge; +2q with −q; a charged plate; a positive and a negative plate facing each other (a parallel-plate capacitor); a point charge above a plate; the charged rod and the charged ring.

Match the field

The orange lines show a field, but the objects that make it are hidden. Choose objects from the trays and place them so that they make the same field. Count the lines: the number of lines leaving or reaching a charge is proportional to its charge. Your own field lines stay hidden while you work; check to see them over the orange ones.
